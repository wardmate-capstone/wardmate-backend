How to read these diagrams

  • Source: the EF Core model of 7 DbContexts across 6 services, inventoried on from docs/diagrams/erd/model-inventory.json. Not yet checked against the live schema on Azure.
  • Solid lines are real SQL foreign keys and only exist inside one database. Dashed lines are REF links between services: each service owns its database, so it stores only the ID, with no FK constraint.
  • Crow's foot notation: two bars mean exactly one, circle + bar means zero or one, circle + crow's foot means zero or many.
  • approved_qrs and approved_at are legacy technical names and do not mean an administrative approval. The current flow ends at DOCUMENTS_READY (documents are ready) and OFFICIALLY_RECEIVED (received at the counter).
  • Boxes with a dashed border and a FUTURE label are 6 planned tables that are not implemented yet: ai_request_logs, ocr_scan_logs, audit_logs, system_settings, service_health_logs, system_backup_logs. Their columns come from the original 35-table specification (docs/diagrams/erd/archive-unified-20261015.md); types are proposals, and NOT NULL, lengths and indexes are not decided. Relationships F1–F4 are planned. knowledge_documents and knowledge_chunks moved from FUTURE to implemented on 24/10/2026 (TASK-28/29). Untick "FUTURE tables" to see only what is implemented.
  • Hangfire tables, the EF migration history, RabbitMQ and Blob Storage are not included.
